Payments veteran Karen Coffey joins Arkonyk as Head of Client Services

Former First Data, Fiserv and Verifi executive brings partner-development and advisory experience to Arkonyk’s Descriptors.com launch.

CLEVELAND, October 20, 2026 — Arkonyk, the payments intelligence company, today announced that Karen Coffey has joined as Head of Client Services. Coffey, founder of Amplify Consulting, LLC, previously held roles at First Data, Fiserv, Visa and Verifi, and will help Arkonyk integrate Descriptors.com to its portfolio of partners and lead platform client support.

Coffey is an executive advisor focused on payments strategy and partner development. At Arkonyk, she will own integration and client support, guiding acquirers, ISOs and merchants from signed agreement to live deployment and serving as their ongoing point of contact. Descriptors.com targets one of the most preventable causes of chargebacks, cardholders who don’t recognize a charge on their statement, and Coffey’s focus is getting clients live quickly and keeping them supported as they grow.

“‘If it ain’t broke, don’t fix it’ is the old saying, but the version that actually moves industries forward is: if it is broken, innovate. I’m excited to see Rick and his team diving in headfirst to reinvent how merchants and consumers tackle an age-old problem. Challenging old norms is nothing new in payments. I recall when aggregators were against the rules and wallets were only in your pocket or purse. Innovation, evolution and a bit of chutzpah keep breaking old mindsets, and Descriptors.com can join the ranks of those that proved that even though we were told it couldn’t be done, it could,” said Coffey.

“I first met Karen when she was at Litle & Co. We had a number of mutual customers, and I kept hearing from them how much they liked and respected her. Customers don’t usually talk about their relationship manager at all, let alone with that kind of loyalty, so I knew Karen was special. Later I was fortunate to bring her onto the Verifi team to lead partner relationships and integrations, and she was outstanding. As we started signing acquiring banks and ISOs to Descriptors.com, I wanted to be sure our partners were successful and had a great relationship with us from day one. Karen was the first person I thought of, and fortunately for us, she said yes,” said Rick Lynch, Founder and CEO of Arkonyk.

About Karen Coffey

Karen Coffey is the founder of Amplify Consulting, LLC, a consultancy focused on using payments to drive revenue and growth. Her career spans executive advisory, strategy and partner development at First Data, Fiserv, Visa and Verifi.

About Arkonyk

Arkonyk is a payments intelligence company on a mission to bring truth to payments. Its flagship product, Descriptors.com, tackles one of the most preventable causes of chargebacks: cardholders who don’t recognize the merchant name on their statement. Descriptors.com is a neutral data layer that maps every billing descriptor to a verified, enriched and authoritative merchant profile.

The platform gives acquirers and ISOs a live risk signal across their portfolios, including consumer search activity, volume, descriptor collisions, friction alerts and compliance issues. Arkonyk also offers a free utility, WhoBilled.me, powered by Descriptors.com, where cardholders can look up any charge. The company’s portfolio also includes FeeSuite, a payments reconciliation platform, and ARKITEKT, its payments advisory practice. Learn more at arkonyk.com or become an early adopter at descriptors.com.
