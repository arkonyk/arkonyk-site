What it does

Reconciliation made easy

Reconcile the activity

Payouts, deposits, fees, reserves, and chargebacks matched automatically — every settlement tied to what actually hit your bank, with clear exception reports.

Verify against your rate schedule

Charged fees are checked against your contracted rates — rate comparisons with overcharges and unexpected changes flagged for recovery.

Review the true cost of payments

Monthly cost by processor, entity, merchant ID, and fee type — your real effective rate, not the headline number on a rate sheet.

Keep the record

Every close preserved with a searchable audit trail and close-ready exports — a durable record of what you were charged, and when, across any number of MIDs.

How it works

A bolt-on — from sign-up to your first reconciled close

1 · Sign up

No integration project and no change to how you accept payments — FeeSuite bolts on alongside your existing setup.

2 · Connect your banks

Bank accounts connect securely through Plaid, and records are masked or tokenized — security-first workflows from day one.

3 · Upload statements

Add your acquirer and processor statements. Fragmented fee data is parsed and normalized into one consistent model.

4 · Run reconciliation

Payouts match to deposits, exceptions are surfaced and labeled, and the close is preserved with a full audit trail — any date range, any number of MIDs.

Chargebacks are tracked until funds return · Offset pairs are netted and resolved · Every period is kept and exportable
