The core insight

Nobody builds their payment stack twice.

Most companies set it up once — early, under pressure, with whoever would say yes — and then never look at it again. Meanwhile volume grows, new markets open up, fraud patterns shift, and processors quietly renegotiate nothing in your favor. The stack that got you to $10M ARR is usually not the stack that should get you to $100M, or even $1B.

When teams call us in

Sound familiar?

“We want to go global, but we’re not global yet.”

Companies expanding into new countries, about to discover that payments abroad isn’t “the same thing plus currency conversion.” Local payment methods, local acquiring, cross-border fraud rules, FX exposure, and regulatory quirks all need architecture — not an afterthought bolted onto a domestic setup.

“We haven’t looked at this in years.”

Established merchants running on legacy pricing, legacy processors, or legacy risk rules nobody has revisited since the deal was signed. They don’t know what they don’t know — and they’re often bleeding basis points and unnecessary chargeback losses without realizing it.
